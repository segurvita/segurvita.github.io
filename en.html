I am an XR engineer with 10 years of experience in Unity, spanning VR/AR development, web technologies, test automation, and engineering leadership. In addition to hands-on design and implementation, I have been responsible for technical decision-making at the team and organizational level.

At STYLY, I served as the lead engineer for the STYLY Studio team. STYLY Studio received the Best Creator & Authoring Tool award at AWE 2024. I have also worked on on-site XR implementations at over 50 locations across Japan (LBE), and built external technical connections through technical knowledge sharing on Qiita and X.
